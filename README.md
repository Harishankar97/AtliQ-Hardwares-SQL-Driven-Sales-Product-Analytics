# AtliQ-Hardwares-SQL-Driven-Sales-Product-Analytics
A hands-on data analytics project focused on using SQL to convert raw business data into meaningful insights for AtliQ Hardwares, a consumer electronics company operating across India and global markets.

# 🚨 **Problem Context**

AtliQ Hardwares generates large volumes of sales and operational data but lacks a structured analytics layer to support fast and informed decision-making.

**Key business questions:**

* How is the product catalog evolving over time?
* Which product categories contribute most to growth?
* How does sales performance fluctuate across months and quarters?
* Is revenue dependent on specific channels or customers?
* Are discount strategies consistent across major clients?

👉 The objective was to bridge the gap between raw data and business decisions using SQL.

# 🎯 **Project Objective**

Build a strong analytical foundation by:

* Structuring raw relational data
* Writing optimized SQL queries
* Extracting insights across products, markets, and customers
* Supporting decision-making with clear data interpretation

# 📂 **Dataset Overview**


The project uses multiple relational tables:

* **fact_sales_monthly** → Monthly sales volume
* **fact_gross_price** → Product pricing details
* **fact_manufacturing_cost** → Cost of production
* **fact_pre_invoice_deductions** → Discount data
* **dim_product** → Product hierarchy and segments
* **dim_customer** → Customer and market information

# 📈 **Key Business Insights**

### 📦 Product Growth Trends
* Product count increased significantly year-over-year
* Growth mainly driven by specific segments like accessories
  👉 Indicates focused expansion rather than balanced diversification
  
### 🧩 Product Distribution
* A large portion of products belong to a few key categories
  👉 Suggests dependency on limited segments

### 🛒 Channel Contribution

* Retail channel dominates overall revenue share
  👉 Highlights reliance on third-party distribution

### 📅 Sales Patterns

* Sales show strong seasonal behavior
* Certain months contribute disproportionately
  👉 Demand is uneven and requires better planning

### 📊 Quarterly Performance

* Some quarters outperform while others lag
  👉 Inventory and supply chain need better alignment

### 🏆 Product-Level Contribution

* A small number of products drive the majority of sales
  👉 Business depends heavily on top-performing SKUs

### 💸 Discount Strategy

* High and consistent discounting across major customers
  👉 Indicates potential margin pressure


### 🏭 Cost Variability

* Significant variation in manufacturing costs across products
  👉 Profitability likely varies widely


### 🌏 Market Reach

* Presence across multiple APAC countries
  👉 Opens opportunities for region-specific strategies
